What are Switch Layers?

A Switch Layer is like a magic flipbook folder in Moho. It holds multiple drawings, but only displays one at a time, allowing you to swap between different shapes or poses quickly.

How to Add Switch Layers in Moho

1
Create a New Switch Layer:
  • Go to your Layers panel.
  • Click the New Layer button (+).
  • Select Switch from the menu.
  • Give the layer a name (e.g., "Mouth").
2
Add Your Drawings:
  • Create or drag your vector layers into the Switch folder.
  • Only the top drawing will be visible on your canvas.
3
Switch Between Drawings:
  • Right-click the Switch Layer folder in your Layers panel or on the Timeline.
  • Select the drawing you want to show from the menu that pops up.
