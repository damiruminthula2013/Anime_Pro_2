Animate 10x Faster in Moho!

Discover 6 magic shortcuts pro animators use so you can skip tedious re-drawing and focus on bringing your wildest cartoon stories to life!

Why it helps: Keeps character limbs smooth and saves hundreds of repetitive drawings!
Kid Tip: Smart Bones work great for arm joints, mouth smiles, and eye winks!

Hack 1: Step-by-Step Smart Bone Setup

12 Easy Steps
1
Open Moho Click File > New for a fresh page.
2
Create Drawing Layer In Layers window, click + and pick Vector Layer (drawing layer). Name it "Arm".
3
Draw Upper Arm Use Add Point Tool (A) to draw the top part of the arm.
4
Draw Lower Arm Draw lower arm next to it, letting edges touch and rest slightly on top of each other.
5
Create Bone Layer Click + and select Bone Layer (skeleton folder). Name it "Rig".
6
Group Layers Drag "Arm" layer inside "Rig" bone folder.
7
Add Bones Draw 2 bones along the arm with Add Bone Tool (A).
8
Glue Drawing to Bones Highlight drawing points and click Bone > Flexi-Bind Points (glues drawing directly to bone).
9
Make Smart Dial Click elbow bone, then menu: Bone > Make Smart Bone Dial.
10
Set Dial Name Type "Elbow Bend" and press OK to enter setup mode.
11
Fix Arm Joint Bend bone and adjust drawing points so elbow looks smooth with no messy bumps!
12
Test on Timeline! Double click Main Timeline. Turn dial on Frame 1—it bends automatically!

Dynamic Bone Spring Physics

Don't manually animate hair bouncing, puppy ears flopping, or superhero capes waving! Turn on Dynamic Physics and Moho handles all the springy movement automatically.

Why it helps: Gives your animations bouncy life automatically without extra keyframes!
Kid Tip: Perfect for ponytails, puppy ears, monster tentacles, and superhero capes!

Hack 2: Step-by-Step Dynamic Physics Setup

11 Easy Steps
1
Pick a Floppy Part Choose hair, ponytail, cape, puppy ears, or animal tail!
2
Draw the Shape On a Drawing layer, draw your ponytail hanging naturally down.
3
Create Bone Chain Add 3 or 4 small bones in a straight line down ponytail (A).
4
Connect the Bones Make sure each bone is linked (like a toy chain) to bone above it (P).
5
Select Hair Bones Hold Shift and click all hair bones (B).
6
Open Bone Settings Press Ctrl + F for Bone Constraints (Bone Rules window).
7
Turn On Magic Spring Check box labeled Dynamic Bone to activate bounce!
8
Set Bounciness Set Spring Force (bounciness amount) to 0.5.
9
Set Smooth Stop Set Damping (smooth slowdown) to 0.2 so hair settles gently.
10
Animate Head Movement Move character's head up & down on main timeline frame 1 to 24.
11
Hit Play! Press Spacebar—ponytail bounces automatically!

Shared Styles (1-Click Color Swap)

Instead of repainting every shirt, hat, and shoe frame individually, link them to a Shared Style color palette! Change the style box once, and your character updates everywhere instantly!

Why it helps: Change entire character color themes in literally 1 second!
Kid Tip: Easily create day/night modes or super-powered glowing forms for your hero!

Hack 3: Step-by-Step Shared Styles Setup

11 Easy Steps
1
Open Style Panel Find Style Window on right side of screen (Ctrl + 1).
2
Click "New Style" Under Styles menu, click New button.
3
Name Your Style Type a clear name like "Shirt Outfit" or "Hero Cape".
4
Pick Fill Color Click Fill Color square color box and pick favorite hero color.
5
Set Outer Border Set stroke width (outer line border) to 4px.
6
Select Character Shirt Use Select Shape Tool (Q) and click shirt.
7
Apply Shared Style In Style drop-down, pick "Shirt Outfit".
8
Link Sleeves & Hat Click shirt sleeves and hat shapes, link them all to "Shirt Outfit"!
9
Double Click Style Anytime you want a new color, double-click "Shirt Outfit".
10
Choose New Color Pick a new color (like Neon Green or Gold) and click OK.
11
Instant Color Swap! ALL shirt parts across entire animation change color in 1 second!

4

Target Bones (Floor Pin Lock)

Stop your character's feet from sliding like they are ice skating! Use a Target Pin to stick their feet firmly to the ground so knees bend automatically when they squat or walk!

Why it helps: Locks feet to the ground automatically so walk animations look natural!
Kid Tip: Essential for jumping, dancing, crouching, and walking cycles!

Hack 4: Step-by-Step Floor Pin Lock Setup

11 Easy Steps
1
Draw Leg Skeleton Draw thigh bone and leg bone using Add Bone Tool (A).
2
Add Floor Target Pin Draw a tiny extra bone right below foot pointing down. Name it "Floor Pin".
3
Unlink Floor Pin Click Floor Pin bone and press P, then click empty space so it floats free.
4
Select Lower Leg Click lower leg bone with Select Bone Tool (B).
5
Open Bone Rules Press Ctrl + F to open Bone Constraints (Bone Rules window).
6
Find Target Menu Look for drop-down menu labeled Target Bone.
7
Pick Floor Pin Choose "Floor Pin" from list to tie leg tip to target bone.
8
Close Popup Click Close to save bone target connection.
9
Grab Body Bone Use Manipulate Bones Tool (Z) and drag body down.
10
Watch Leg Bend! The foot stays glued to ground while knee bends automatically!
11
Zero Foot Slipping! Your character can now jump and step cleanly without sliding!

Switch Layers (Talking Mouth Swapper)

Group all your character's mouth shapes (Smile, O-mouth, Happy, Surprised) into a single Switch Magic Folder! Click any mouth shape to swap drawings in 1 second when talking.

Why it helps: Swap complex mouth and hand drawings instantly with no re-drawing!
Kid Tip: Great for character dialogue, hand gestures (fist, wave, peace sign), and eye states!

Hack 5: Step-by-Step Switch Folder Setup

11 Easy Steps
1
Open Layers Window Click + button at top of Layers panel.
2
Select Switch Folder Choose Switch (Magic Swapper Folder) from drop-down menu.
3
Name Your Folder Type "Mouth Shapes" as folder name.
4
Add First Mouth Layer Inside folder, add new Drawing Layer named "Smile".
5
Draw Big Smile Draw a happy smiling mouth shape on this layer.
6
Duplicate Layer Right-click "Smile" layer and click Duplicate Layer (copy layer).
7
Rename & Change Mouth Rename to "O-Mouth" and adjust drawing into a open round "O".
8
Add More Mouths Repeat steps to create "Surprised" and "Talking" mouth drawings!
9
Go To Timeline Frame 1 Move playback needle to frame 1 on timeline.
10
Right-Click Folder Right-click "Mouth Shapes" folder on timeline and choose any mouth!
11
Instant Talking! Switch mouth shapes along timeline—your character talks smoothly!

6

Actions Library (Save Your Best Moves)

Animate a wave, jump, or dance move once and save it into your Actions Secret Box! Paste that exact movement anytime anywhere in your cartoon with 1 simple click!

Why it helps: Reuse complex walking, waving, and dancing animations infinitely!
Kid Tip: Build your own reusable library of cool superhero moves and victory dances!

Hack 6: Step-by-Step Action Library Setup

11 Easy Steps
1
Open Actions Window Press Ctrl + K to open Actions Panel.
2
Click "New Action" Click top right menu icon inside Actions window and pick New Action.
3
Name Your Move Type a fun move title like "Happy Hand Wave".
4
Enter Action Space Notice timeline turns red—you are inside secret motion editor!
5
Animate Hand Wave Move arm left and right between frame 1 and 20.
6
Return to Main Show Double-click Main Timeline at top of Actions list.
7
Choose Timeline Frame Click frame 40 where you want character to wave.
8
Select Saved Move Single-click "Happy Hand Wave" inside Actions window.
9
Click "Insert Reference" Click Insert Reference button at top of panel.
10
Instant Motion Paste! Your complete hand wave animation appears instantly on timeline!
11
Reuse 100 Times! Paste your wave anywhere in your cartoon with zero extra drawing work!
