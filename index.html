🦴 Multi-Layer Stickman & Bones Guide (10 Simple Steps)

Follow these simple steps to draw each body part on its own layer and attach bones easily!

Easy 10-Step Method
Step 1 Bone Folder

1. Create Master Bone Folder

Click + New Layer and select Bone Layer. Name it "Stickman Character". This folder will hold all drawing layers inside!

Step 2 Layer 1: Head

2. Draw Head on "Head Layer"

Create a Vector layer named "Head" inside the bone folder. Draw the face circle, eyes, and smile here!

Step 3 Layer 2: Torso

3. Draw Body Spine on "Torso Layer"

Create a new Vector layer named "Torso". Draw the vertical line for the body chest and spine line.

Step 4 Layer 3 & 4: Arms

4. Draw Arms on Separate Arm Layers

Create two separate layers: "Left Arm" and "Right Arm". Draw straight arm lines on each layer starting from the shoulder!

Step 5 Layer 5 & 6: Legs

5. Draw Legs on Separate Leg Layers

Create layers named "Left Leg" and "Right Leg". Draw leg lines down from the waist hip area!

Step 6 Add Bone Tool: A

6. Add Spine & Head Bones

Select the Bone Folder, press Key "A" (Add Bone). Click at the waist and drag up to neck (Spine Bone). Drag again from neck to top of head (Head Bone)!

Step 7 Arm & Leg Bones

7. Add Limb Bones

Drag bones from shoulder to hand for arms, and hip to foot for legs. Link each arm and leg bone back to the spine bone!

Step 8 Bone Strength: S

8. Adjust Bone Strength Radius

Press Key "S" and drag left to shrink the glowing red strength bubbles so each bone only influences its own layer!

Step 9 Flexi-Bind

9. Bind Layers to Bones

Select all bones (`Ctrl + A`) and click Bone > Flexi-Bind Layer in the top menu. This glues your drawing lines to the skeleton!

Step 10 Manipulate: Z

10. Animate Live!

Press Key "Z" (Manipulate Tool) and drag any bone! Your stickman puppet will wave, jump, run, and dance!
