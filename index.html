1

The Smart Bones Control Dial

Instead of re-drawing face expressions or elbow bends over and over, turn them into a magic turn dial! Turn the dial, and Moho automatically controls the drawing for you.

Why it helps: Keeps character limbs smooth and saves hundreds of repetitive drawings!
Kid Tip: Smart Bones work great for arm joints, mouth smiles, and eye winks!

Hack 1: Step-by-Step Smart Bone Setup

12 Easy Steps
1
Open Moho Click File > New (or Ctrl + N) for a fresh page.
2
Create Drawing Layer In Layers window, click + and pick Vector Layer (this is your drawing layer!). Name it "Arm".
3
Draw Upper Arm Use Add Point Tool (A) to draw the top part of the arm.
4
Draw Lower Arm Draw the lower arm next to it, letting the edges touch and rest slightly on top of each other at the elbow joint.
5
Create Bone Layer Click + and select Bone Layer (this folder holds your skeleton!). Name it "Rig".
6
Group Layers Drag your "Arm" drawing layer inside the "Rig" bone folder.
7
Add Bones Select Bone layer and draw 2 bones along the arm with Add Bone Tool (A).
8
Glue Drawing to Bones Highlight drawing points and click Bone > Flexi-Bind Points (this glues your drawing directly to the bone!).
9
Make Smart Dial Click elbow bone, then top menu: Bone > Make Smart Bone Dial.
10
Set Dial Name Type "Elbow Bend" and press OK to open special setup mode.
11
Fix Arm Joint Bend the bone and adjust drawing points so the elbow looks neat with no messy bumps!
12
Test on Timeline! Double click Main Timeline. Turn your dial on Frame 1—it bends automatically like magic!

2

Dynamic Bone Spring Physics

Don't manually animate hair bouncing, puppy ears flopping, or superhero capes waving! Turn on Dynamic Physics and Moho handles all the springy movement automatically.

Why it helps: Gives your animations bouncy life automatically without extra keyframes!
Kid Tip: Perfect for ponytails, puppy ears, monster tentacles, and superhero capes!

Hack 2: Step-by-Step Dynamic Physics Setup

11 Easy Steps
1
Pick a Floppy Part Choose hair, ponytail, cape, puppy ears, or animal tail!
2
Draw the Shape On a Drawing (Vector) layer, draw your ponytail hanging naturally down.
3
Create Bone Chain Add 3 or 4 small bones in a straight line down the ponytail (A).
4
Connect the Bones Make sure each bone is linked (like a toy chain) to the bone right above it (P).
5
Select Hair Bones Hold Shift and click all hair bones with Select Bone (B).
6
Open Bone Settings Press Ctrl + F to open Bone Constraints (Bone Rules window).
7
Turn On Magic Spring Check the box labeled Dynamic Bone to turn on bounce movement!
8
Set Bounciness Change Spring Force (bounciness amount) to 0.5 for extra wiggle.
9
Set Smooth Stop Set Damping (smooth slowdown) to 0.2 so hair settles gently.
10
Animate Head Movement Move character's head up & down on main timeline frame 1 to 24.
11
Hit Play! Press Spacebar—the ponytail bounces automatically with physics!

3

Shared Styles (1-Click Color Swap)

Instead of repainting every shirt, hat, and shoe frame individually, link them to a Shared Style color palette! Change the style box once, and your character updates everywhere instantly!

Why it helps: Change entire character color themes in literally 1 second!
Kid Tip: Easily create day/night modes or super-powered glowing forms for your hero!

Hack 3: Step-by-Step Shared Styles Setup

11 Easy Steps
1
Open Style Panel Find the Style Window on the right side of screen (Ctrl + 1).
2
Click "New Style" Under the Styles drop-down menu, click the New button.
3
Name Your Style Type a clear name like "Shirt Outfit" or "Hero Cape".
4
Pick Fill Color Click the Fill Color square box and pick your favorite hero color.
5
Set Outer Border Set stroke width (outer line border) to 4px with a dark line.
6
Select Character Shirt Use Select Shape Tool (Q) and click character shirt.
7
Apply Shared Style In Style window drop-down, pick "Shirt Outfit".
8
Link Sleeves & Hat Click shirt sleeves and hat shapes, link them all to "Shirt Outfit"!
9
Double Click Style Anytime you want a new color, double-click "Shirt Outfit" in list.
10
Choose New Color Pick a new color (like Neon Green or Gold) and click OK.
11
Instant Color Swap! ALL shirt parts across the entire animation change color in 1 second!
