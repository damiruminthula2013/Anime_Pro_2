4 Easy Steps to Download Free Media

1

Open Pixabay

Open Google Search and type Pixabay or go directly to pixabay.com.

2

Choose Media Type

Click on the category tab: Photos, Illustrations, Vectors, Videos, Music, or Sound Effects.

3

Search & Select Asset

Type your desired search terms (e.g. "car", "nature", "flower") and click on your preferred result to open details.

4

Download Quality File

Click the green Download button, pick your preferred resolution (e.g., 4K, 1080p, SVG), and save to your computer.
